Fortunal DebtManager

Know who owes. Know when. Get paid.

Fortunal DebtManager is a mobile-first debt and payment management platform designed for individuals, freelancers, and small businesses to keep track of money owed, payments received, outstanding balances, and overdue debts.

Instead of relying on notebooks, spreadsheets, or scattered messages, DebtManager brings customer debts and payment records into one organized dashboard.

Key Features

- 📊 Dashboard — View total money owed, overdue debts, upcoming payments, and money collected.
- 👥 Customer Management — Store and manage customer information and their financial history.
- 💰 Debt Tracking — Record debts, due dates, items/services, notes, and payment methods.
- 💳 Partial Payments — Record multiple payments against a single debt and automatically calculate the remaining balance.
- 📱 M-PESA Support — Designed for Kenyan payment workflows, including M-PESA payment tracking and Safaricom Daraja integration.
- 🧾 Receipts — Generate professional payment receipts after valid payments are recorded.
- 📄 Statements & Reports — View customer ledgers, payment history, outstanding balances, and collection reports.
- 🔔 Payment Reminders — Prepare personalized reminders for WhatsApp and SMS.
- 🤖 AI Entry Assistant — Convert natural-language or voice descriptions into structured debt/payment records, with confirmation before saving.
- 📅 Payment Plans — Support installments and recurring payment schedules.
- 🔐 Secure Architecture — Sensitive API credentials are kept on the backend rather than exposed in the frontend.
- 📱 Progressive Web App — Designed for mobile-first use while remaining accessible on larger screens.

Built For

DebtManager is designed for:

- Small businesses
- Retailers and shops
- Freelancers
- Service providers
- Tutors and educators
- Individuals managing personal debts
- Businesses that sell products or services on credit

Kenyan-First Approach

DebtManager uses Kenyan Shillings (KES) as the default currency and is designed around payment methods commonly used by Kenyan users, including:

M-PESA • Cash • Bank • Airtel Money • Card • Cheque • Other

The platform is designed to grow into a broader payment and collection ecosystem while keeping financial records separate from payment-provider credentials and transactions.

Financial Integrity

DebtManager calculates balances from persisted payment records:

Outstanding Balance = Original Debt − Valid Payments Received

Payments are recorded individually, allowing users to maintain an auditable history of transactions and partial payments.

Project Status

DebtManager is under active development as a SaaS/PWA product under the Fortunal brand.

The project is being developed with a focus on practical debt management, payment tracking, receipts, reminders, AI-assisted data entry, and future payment integrations.
